    THE ALGORITHM Why the short route wins +

    Each field stores an undirected amount on an edge and a scalar concentration at each endpoint. Ants never deposit an arrow; the visible arrow is derived from the weaker endpoint toward the stronger one. Every ant initially explores, extending a persistent concentration gradient away from the hill.

    P(edge) = weight(edge) / Σ weight(options)

    The first carrier climbs the persistent hill gradient while extending a short-lived gradient from food. Later outbound ants climb that food gradient and carriers descend the same field back to the hill. A temporary novelty choice can try a less-covered edge; there is no scout caste or remembered route. Equal-speed ants renew short routes more often. Add or move food and the old field fades while the uninterrupted colony adapts.
