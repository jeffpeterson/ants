    THE ALGORITHM Why the short route wins +

    Each pheromone is one scalar level per node. At a junction, an ant measures each branch at its opposite endpoint. Edge shading and arrows interpolate the endpoint levels; the arrow shows concentration slope, not an automatic destination.

    P(edge) = weight(edge) / Σ weight(options)

    The controls mix endpoint strength, optional polarity, local heading, edge length, and U-turn cost independently for outbound and homebound ants. Scouting is a random mode with a stochastic exit chance. Only ants visibly carrying food deposit food signal. No ant stores a route or visited set. Short routes are renewed more often because every ant moves at one physical speed.
