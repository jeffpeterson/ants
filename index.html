    THE ALGORITHM Why the short route wins +

    Exploring ants mark the reverse arc of each crossing, creating a local breadcrumb field toward the hill. Before food is known, ants take turns making finite discovery tours on less-covered, unvisited local edges. Others wait for foodward signal. A carrier follows adjacent hillward coverage along its own loop-free breadcrumbs and lays food signal on the opposite arc.

    P(edge) = weight(edge) / Σ weight(options)

    On later trips every ant normally samples the foodward distribution, but can briefly try a less-covered branch before returning to the signal. Failed attempts retrace their own breadcrumbs. There is no permanent scout caste. Short routes receive more deposits per minute because equal-speed ants finish more round trips. Add or move food and the old signal will fade while the uninterrupted colony adapts.
